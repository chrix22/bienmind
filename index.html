Our Standards

What makes a BienMind-published service

We apply the same rigour to AI services that a great editor brings to a manuscript — insisting on truth, clarity, and genuine value for the reader. Our framework is built on five pillars.

Submit Your Service
  1. 01

    Evidence & Efficacy

    Claims must be grounded in credible research. We cross-reference scientific literature and consult domain experts.

  2. 02

    Data Ethics & Privacy

    We scrutinise data collection, consent practices, and how sensitive wellness data is stored, used, and shared.

  3. 03

    Inclusivity & Accessibility

    Services must work for — and not harm — diverse populations, including those with disabilities or limited digital literacy.

  4. 04

    Transparency & Explainability

    AI decision-making should be understandable to users. We require clear disclosures on how recommendations are generated.

  5. 05

    Mission Alignment

    Every published service must genuinely serve human or planetary wellbeing — not exploit it.
